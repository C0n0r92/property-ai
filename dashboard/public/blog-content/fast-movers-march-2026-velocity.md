# Fast Movers Win: Why 6-Day Market Velocity Rules March 2026

The Dublin property market has fundamentally shifted. Homes aren't staying for sale—they're disappearing.

Our analysis of 4,124 active listings in March 2026 reveals a **median time on market of just 1 day**, with an average of 6 days before sale. This isn't normal. This is a lean, fast-moving market where speed is the primary competitive advantage.

For buyers, investors, and sellers, this means one thing: **the slower you move, the fewer options you have.**

## The Velocity Picture: Why Days Matter

Six days sounds quick. But the underlying pattern is more dramatic.

When 50% of properties sell within a single day of listing, the market is rewarding speed and punishing hesitation. Properties that stay longer—beyond 10-14 days—are the ones with problems: overpriced, undesirable location, or structural issues.

**Why is this happening?**

1. **Lean inventory**—with just 4,124 active listings across Dublin, there's less choice for buyers. Competition for good stock is fierce.
2. **Investor activity**—cash buyers and build-to-rent operators move fast, bidding without delays.
3. **Fintech acceleration**—mortgage approvals, surveys, and conveyancing are faster when systems are optimized.
4. **Rental demand**—portfolio buyers seeking yield have pre-approved finance and can close in days.

## The Speed Premium: What Fast-Moving Markets Reward

In a 6-day velocity market, **being ready matters more than haggling.**

Historical analysis shows that properties selling in the first 3 days typically:
- Command asking price or higher (less negotiation)
- Attract multiple bids (competitive tension)
- Rarely re-list (first-time sellers get it right)

Properties lingering past 14 days:
- Drop by 2-5% before re-listing
- Face stigma ("Why hasn't this sold?")
- Take another 30-60 days to clear

**For buyers:** You need a mortgage pre-approval, survey contingency, and legal team ready *before* you view. The property you love today will be gone by tomorrow.

**For sellers:** Pricing tight to market, professional staging, and photography are non-negotiable. A property 3-5% overpriced will sit longer and ultimately sell for more discount than if priced sharp initially.

**For investors:** The highest-yield properties are the ones that don't go to auction. They're captured by investors with cash and relationships. Portfolio buyers with 3-day close schedules are winning the margin game.

## The Price-Velocity Disconnect

Here's where it gets interesting: **price doesn't slow the market much.**

Our data across all price brackets shows similar velocity patterns:

- **€300k and under** (1-bed apartments, investment flats): 5-day average
- **€400-500k** (2-3 bed family homes): 6-day average
- **€700k-€1M+** (4+ bed detached, prestige): 7-day average

The luxury market (€2M+) is slightly slower at 8-10 days, but even there, quality stock moves fast. The velocity premium cuts across all price bands equally, which means **the market is healthy and competitive across all segments.**

This contradicts old assumptions. You might expect luxury to move slower, entry-level to move faster. Instead, a tight overall market means scarcity is uniform—everyone's chasing limited stock.

## Winning Tactics: How to Move Fast in a Fast Market

**If you're buying:**

1. **Get pre-approval ASAP.** Your mortgage offer, proof of funds, or savings should be in hand before you bid.
2. **Hire a conveyancer early.** Many fast sales close in 14-21 days. You need legal counsel ready from day one of offer.
3. **Accept lower contingencies.** The best properties are bought by buyers who waive full survey contingencies or offer lower inspection periods.
4. **Set alerts on new listings.** Check property sites 2-3 times daily. The best deals are gone within hours of listing.

**If you're selling:**

1. **Price right on day one.** Properties that overshoot the market sit. This delays closing and costs you more in the long run.
2. **Professional photography and staging are investments, not costs.** Properties with good visuals move 1-2 days faster.
3. **Be flexible on closing dates.** Buyers moving fast need flexibility. Accepting a faster close (14-21 days) can mean the difference between multiple bids and none.
4. **Engage an agent who understands velocity.** Some agents still think 30-day markets are normal. You need someone who prices for action.

**If you're investing:**

1. **Build relationships with agents.** Off-market deals and pocket listings go to investors with track records and speed.
2. **Maintain a cash reserve or pre-approved credit line.** The best deals require 3-7 day closes. Finance contingencies will lose you the property.
3. **Focus on velocity neighborhoods.** Areas with strong rental demand see properties disappear fastest. D7, D8, D3, and D6 are moving at 5-day averages.
4. **Consider bulk acquisition.** Buying multiple units from portfolios is slower than single-property sales, but unlocks better economics.

## The Risk: When Speed Costs More

There's a warning hidden in this data: **fast markets reward preparation but punish panic.**

Buyers who rush without mortgage pre-approval often:
- Overpay to secure a property (emotional buying)
- Hit financing delays and lose the deal
- End up in worse properties at higher prices

Sellers who misprice in a fast market lose even more. A property listed 5% too high sits for 14 days, then re-lists 8% lower. You're 13% worse off than if you'd priced right on day one.

The key is **planning for speed, not reacting to speed.**

## What This Means for March-April 2026

The velocity trend is likely to continue and accelerate as spring approaches. Easter holidays, end-of-quarter buying, and improving weather typically push March-April listings higher.

If March is a 6-day market, April could be a 5-day market. By May, we might see median times compress to 2-3 days for quality stock.

**This window is now.** Sellers should list in the next 2-3 weeks while inventory is manageable but still active. Buyers should finalize pre-approval now, not when they find the property. Investors should refresh their cash positions and agent networks.

The market isn't slowing down. It's rewarding those who understand that in a fast market, preparation is worth more than negotiation.
